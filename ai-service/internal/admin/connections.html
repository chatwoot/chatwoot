What a connected AI can do

  • Read only: list documents with status and usage, read one, find near-copies, read the business profile, list website pages and data files, see recent changes, and ask the bot a test question.
  • Read and write also: suggest a new or changed document (by its key, for example product-017) or a change to the business name, about, tone and rules. These wait for your approval on AI approvals; customers keep seeing the current text until you approve.
  • Deleting a document, and syncing or rescheduling a website page or data file, take effect at once (a data file syncs in the background). Deletes and schedule changes show under Recent changes on Home as the AI tool; what a sync changes shows as “website sync”. The history is kept for 180 days by default.
