# Product Overview

Chatwoot is an open-source customer support platform that serves as an alternative to Intercom, Zendesk, and Salesforce Service Cloud. It provides businesses with a modern, self-hosted solution for managing customer conversations across multiple channels.

## Core Features

- **Omnichannel Support**: Centralizes conversations from live chat, email, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, WhatsApp, Telegram, Line, SMS, and more
- **AI Agent (Captain)**: Automated response system for handling common queries and reducing agent workload
- **Help Center Portal**: Self-service knowledge base with articles, FAQs, and guides
- **Team Collaboration**: Private notes, @mentions, labels, canned responses, and auto-assignment
- **Customer Management**: Contact profiles, segmentation, custom attributes, and interaction history
- **Integrations**: Slack, Dialogflow, Shopify, Google Translate, Linear tickets, and dashboard apps
- **Analytics & Reports**: Conversation, agent, inbox, CSAT reports with real-time monitoring

## Key Integrations

The platform heavily integrates with external services through:
- **SocialWise Flow**: Custom integration for enhanced social media management
- **Dialogflow**: Chatbot automation and natural language processing
- **Instagram/Facebook**: Social media channel management
- **WhatsApp Business API**: Rich messaging and interactive components
- **Slack**: Team collaboration and conversation management

## Architecture Philosophy

Chatwoot follows a modular, service-oriented architecture with clear separation between channels, integrations, and core business logic. The system is designed for scalability and supports both cloud and self-hosted deployments.
